# Tutor: Number or parseInt

## Ladder

- "Which pair surprised you? Is one of them stricter?"
- "Where does parseInt stop reading? Look at the MDN page."

## Typical mistakes

- Thinking an empty field is safe. Ask: is `0` the right value for "nothing typed"?

## What if

- Which one would you use for a price field? For "12px" from CSS?

## Tips

- In practice: `Number` for form input, then check for NaN.
