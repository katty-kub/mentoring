# Tutor: plus or minus

## Ladder

- "Which line surprised you? What type is each side of it?"
- "Which operator also works on text? What does it do with text?"

## Typical mistakes

- Running it before guessing. Ask her to write all 4 guesses first.

## What if

- What does `"5" + 1 + 1` print? And `1 + 1 + "5"`?

## Tips

- `+` glues text. `-`, `*`, `/` turn text into numbers. That's why `+` is the dangerous one.
