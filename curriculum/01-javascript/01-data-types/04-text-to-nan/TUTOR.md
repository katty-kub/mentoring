# Tutor: text to NaN

Her question: "What is NaN?"

## Ladder

- "Which tool turned text into a number in text-to-number? Try it on "abc"."
- "You printed NaN. Now print its type. Surprised?"

## Typical mistakes

- Thinking NaN is text, or an error. It's a number value that means "not a valid number".

## What if

- What does `Number("")` give? And `Number(" 7 ")`?

## Tips

- In apps: a price field with "12 €" becomes NaN. Then the shop shows "NaN €".
